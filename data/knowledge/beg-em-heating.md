# BEG EM: heating grants (KfW 458)
Source: https://www.kfw.de/inlandsfoerderung/Privatpersonen/Bestehende-Immobilie/Förderprodukte/Heizungsförderung-für-Privatpersonen-Wohngebäude-(458)/

## What is funded
KfW programme 458 (Heizungsförderung) funds replacing a heating system with one that runs on renewable energy, for example an air-to-water or ground-source heat pump, a biomass boiler, a connection to a heating network, or solar thermal. The grant covers the device, installation, and needed work such as radiators or a buffer tank.

## Funding rates since 21 July 2026
The base grant is 30 % of eligible costs for all applicants, including landlords and companies. Owner-occupiers who replace a working fossil heating system can add a climate speed bonus (Klimageschwindigkeitsbonus) of 16 %; it falls by 4 percentage points every six months from 1 February 2027 and ends on 1 August 2028. Owner-occupiers with a taxable household income up to 40,000 euros can get an income bonus of up to 40 %. The total rate is capped at 70 %, or 80 % for the lowest incomes. The 5 % efficiency bonus for heat pumps ended on 21 July 2026. Landlords of rented flats usually receive only the 30 % base rate.

## Eligible cost caps
For residential buildings: 28,000 euros for the first dwelling unit, 15,000 euros each for units 2 to 6, and 8,000 euros for each unit from the 7th. The cap for the first unit falls by 750 euros every six months until it reaches 22,000 euros from 1 August 2030.

## Upcoming changes
From the first quarter of 2027, the base rate for heat pumps is planned to drop to 15 % with a 15 % value-creation bonus for heat pumps made in the EU. From 1 July 2027 heat pumps need a digital interface under § 14a EnWG and a smart meter. Switching from district heating to another renewable system will no longer be funded from 2027.
