# Whole-building renovation: Efficiency House (KfW 261)
Source: https://www.kfw.de/inlandsfoerderung/Privatpersonen/Bestehende-Immobilie/Förderprodukte/Bundesförderung-für-effiziente-Gebäude-Wohngebäude-Kredit-(261-262)/

## What it is
Instead of funding single measures, KfW 261 funds a complete renovation to an Efficiency House standard (Effizienzhaus, EH) such as EH 85, EH 70, EH 55 or EH 40. The number shows the primary energy demand compared with a new reference building: EH 55 needs 55 % of the reference. Funding is a low-interest loan with a repayment grant (Tilgungszuschuss).

## Rules since 21 July 2026
The maximum loan is 150,000 euros per dwelling unit. A renewable energy share of at least 65 % is now mandatory, and the separate 5 % renewables bonus was removed. Repayment grants were cut by 10 percentage points and replaced by cheaper interest. A worst-performing-building bonus of 10 % applies to EH 70 to EH 40 when the building starts in the worst efficiency segment. A serial renovation bonus (SerSan) of 5 % exists for prefabricated renovation to EH 70 EE or better.

## Single measures or whole building?
Single measures (BEG EM) can be done step by step and fit owners with limited budgets. A full Efficiency House renovation is usually cheaper per kWh saved because the work is coordinated, but the upfront investment is larger. Since July 2026, BEG EM and Efficiency House funding for the same building can only be combined three years after the other funding ended.
