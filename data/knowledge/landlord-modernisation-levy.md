# Passing retrofit costs to tenants (§ 559 BGB)
Source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__559.html

## The modernisation levy
After an energy modernisation, a landlord may raise the annual rent by up to 8 % of the modernisation costs (Modernisierungsumlage). Costs for repairs that were due anyway (Instandhaltung) and grants received must be deducted first.

## Caps
Within six years the monthly rent may rise by at most 3 euros per m² of living space, or 2 euros per m² if the rent before was below 7 euros per m². For a new heating system under § 559e BGB a landlord can apply 10 % instead of 8 % if they used a grant, capped at 0.50 euros per m² per month.

## Why it matters for planning
Grants lower the amount that can be passed on, but they lower the landlord's own investment more. Planning tools should show the net cost after grants and the maximum rent increase, so owners can compare scenarios. This tool shows a simplified estimate and is not legal advice.
