# Typical retrofit measures for multi-family buildings
Source: https://www.co2online.de/modernisieren-und-bauen/

## Envelope first
Heat escapes mainly through walls, roof, windows and the cellar ceiling. Insulating the envelope first lowers the heat demand, so a smaller and cheaper heat pump can be installed later and runs more efficiently at lower flow temperatures.

## Indicative costs and savings (2026, demo values)
Facade insulation with an external thermal insulation composite system (ETICS, WDVS): 180 to 260 euros per m² of wall, saving 15 to 25 % of heating energy. Pitched roof insulation: 200 to 320 euros per m² of roof, saving 8 to 15 %. Top floor ceiling insulation: 40 to 90 euros per m², saving 6 to 12 %, often the cheapest measure per kWh saved. Basement ceiling insulation: 40 to 80 euros per m², saving 4 to 8 %. Triple-glazed windows: 700 to 1,100 euros per m² of window, saving 8 to 15 %. Ventilation with heat recovery: 6,000 to 9,000 euros per flat. These are rough market ranges; a site visit sets the real price.

## Heat pumps in multi-family buildings
Air-to-water heat pumps cost about 1,800 to 2,600 euros per kW of heating power including installation. Size depends on the heat demand after envelope measures. Radiators may need replacing if flow temperatures above 55 °C are needed.

## Solar PV
Rooftop PV costs about 1,300 to 1,700 euros per kWp. PV is not funded by BEG grants but can be financed with the KfW 270 loan. A tenant electricity model (Mieterstrom) can raise self-consumption.
