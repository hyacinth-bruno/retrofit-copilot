# Reading a German energy certificate (Energieausweis)
Source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/

## Demand or consumption certificate
Under the Building Energy Act (Gebäudeenergiegesetz, GEG) there are two types. A demand certificate (Bedarfsausweis) calculates energy needs from the building's construction and heating system. A consumption certificate (Verbrauchsausweis) is based on measured consumption over three years, so it depends on how residents heat. Demand values are better for planning a retrofit.

## Final energy and primary energy
Final energy (Endenergie) is the energy delivered to the building, such as gas or electricity, in kWh per square metre and year. Primary energy (Primärenergie) also counts energy lost in production and transport. The efficiency class on residential certificates is based on final energy.

## Efficiency classes
Classes by final energy in kWh/(m²·a): A+ below 30, A 30 to under 50, B 50 to under 75, C 75 to under 100, D 100 to under 130, E 130 to under 160, F 160 to under 200, G 200 to under 250, H 250 and above. Many buildings from the 1950s to 1970s without retrofit are in classes F to H.

## Useful area
The reference area is the building's useful floor area (Gebäudenutzfläche, AN), which is calculated from the heated volume and is usually larger than the living area (Wohnfläche).
