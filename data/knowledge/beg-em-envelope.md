# BEG EM: grants for envelope and plant measures (BAFA)
Source: https://www.bafa.de/DE/Energie/Effiziente_Gebaeude/Sanierung_Wohngebaeude/sanierung_wohngebaeude_node.html

## What is funded
The federal programme for efficient buildings, individual measures (Bundesförderung für effiziente Gebäude, Einzelmaßnahmen, BEG EM) gives grants for insulating the building envelope and for plant technology other than heating. Envelope measures include facade insulation, roof insulation, insulation of the top floor ceiling and the basement ceiling, and replacing windows and external doors. Plant technology includes ventilation systems with heat recovery. The Federal Office for Economic Affairs and Export Control (BAFA) handles these grants. An energy efficiency expert (Energieeffizienz-Experte) must be involved.

## Funding rate since 21 July 2026
The base grant is 15 % of eligible costs. A bonus of 5 percentage points is available when the measure is part of an individual renovation roadmap (individueller Sanierungsfahrplan, iSFP). Since the reform of 21 July 2026, the iSFP bonus only applies to the part of the investment above the minimum volume, which is the cap without iSFP and at least 30,000 euros. For small projects under 30,000 euros the effective rate is therefore 15 %.

## Eligible cost caps per building and year
Caps apply per calendar year and building. For a multi-family building without iSFP: 30,000 euros for the first dwelling unit, 15,000 euros each for units 2 to 6, and 8,000 euros for each unit from the 7th. With an iSFP: 60,000 euros for the first unit, 30,000 euros for units 2 to 6 and 15,000 euros from unit 7. Example from the reform summary: a building with 8 dwelling units has a cap of 121,000 euros without iSFP and 240,000 euros with iSFP.

## Planned changes
From the first quarter of 2027 a 5 % worst-performing-building bonus (WPB) is planned for envelope insulation, but not windows, when an iSFP or funded energy advice exists. Funding rules change often; always check the current BAFA guidance before applying.
