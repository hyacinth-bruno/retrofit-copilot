# CO2 emission factors for heating
Source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/anlage_9.html

## Factors used in this tool
Annex 9 of the GEG gives emission factors in grams of CO2 equivalent per kWh of final energy. This tool uses: natural gas 240 g/kWh, heating oil 310 g/kWh, grid electricity 560 g/kWh, wood and pellets 20 g/kWh. District heating varies by network; the tool assumes 280 g/kWh unless the operator states a value.

## Why a heat pump lowers emissions despite the electricity factor
A heat pump delivers about three kWh of heat per kWh of electricity (seasonal performance factor around 3). One kWh of useful heat from gas causes roughly 240 / 0.9 = 267 g, from a heat pump roughly 560 / 3 = 187 g, and less every year as the grid gets greener. With rooftop solar the share of grid electricity drops further.
